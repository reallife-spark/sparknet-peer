## Step 8 complete — inter-node sharing and the governance regime

The last build-out step. Two users on two nodes share knowledge and sessions
under a regime that **defaults to quarantine**, and the whole thing is a pure
in-process engine — no sockets, no keys, no second node required to prove the
logic.

### What landed

`peer-node/services/sparknet-share-regime.py` — the share conversation as a
message-dispatched state machine (`share.offer/accept/ratify/use/reconcile/close`).
It reads the **machine contracts** as its only rule source and refuses to run
without them:

- `contracts/capabilities.json` — `share_types` (kb-view copies=false,
  kb-share copies=true, session-view sensitive, session-handoff
  copies+sensitive, full includes all four lesser and is the only type with
  `includes`) and `share_clocks` (accept 1 h, idle 20 m, ratification 2 h
  master_governed-only, absolute 10 h).
- `contracts/overlay.json` — `governance_mode` (solo=1, peer_pair=2,
  master_governed=3+), the data-plane stanza fields (mtu 1400, keepalive 25,
  config 0600), and the authority-plane one-address rule.
- `contracts/ports.json` — share-intake 4201, session-transfer 4202,
  share-ratification 4203 (all spec-only, no listener).

### DoD map

- **1–4** — `offer()` opens a 1 h invite; `accept()` requires the receiver to
  name a type and enforces **narrowing-only** (`full.includes` is the only
  partial order); the five types come from the contract; temporary vs permanent
  differ on idle (both end at the absolute cap); all four clocks
  (1 h/20 m/2 h/10 h) default from the contract and are **clock-injected**, so
  the tests run in seconds.
- **5–6** — `mode_for()` recomputes governance from the registry node count on
  **every** request (never cached): `solo` (drafts allowed, acceptance refused
  `409 solo`), `peer_pair` (symmetric, both endpoints `flagged`, **no**
  ratification clock — a Master arbitration is refused `409 no-arbiter`),
  `master_governed` (3+) where a Master `ratify` moves both endpoints to
  `authenticated` with `flagged` retained. A third node joining mid-share
  re-evaluates the live share (`re_evaluated` + `re-evaluate` action) instead of
  grandfathering it. `kb-share`/`full` reconciliation is additions+conflicts
  only — a divergent page is a review item, never a silent overwrite.
- **7–8** — `render_stanza()` emits a declarative, time-boxed **data-plane**
  stanza (by-kind port 4201/4202, mtu 1400, keepalive 25, config 0600, single
  `/32` AllowedIPs, nftables fragment) and returns the **authority-plane
  one-address invariant** (`10.100.0.1/32`) unchanged; the host acts on the
  returned actions (`stanza-up`, `teardown-stanza`, `isolate-data-plane`,
  `re-evaluate`). Non-ratification (2 h silence, or a late answer forced to
  refusal) severs the fabric data plane (`isolate-data-plane`) while
  peer-to-peer continues; every share ends at the 10 h absolute cap.

### Wiring (skill contract change)

The bridge's `/session/:id/share` verbs now delegate to the engine:
`SHARE_KINDS` is the five contract types, `_share_regime()` builds a `Regime`
with `now=self._now` and `node_count` from the registry → `solo` on this host.
So a share created here is an honest **invite** (a 1 h draft), not a false
`open`. Step 6's `test_share_refuses_kinds_that_belong_to_step8` is replaced by
`test_share_verbs_now_accept_all_five_step8_kinds`; the Step 6 endpoint test now
asserts `status == "invite"`.
