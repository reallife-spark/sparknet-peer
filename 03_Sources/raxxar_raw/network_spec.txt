   Sparknet Network Topology & Parts List
   Engineering Specification & Bill of Materials for NVIDIA DGX Spark Infrastructure

1. Executive Summary & Architecture Overview
Sparknet deploys departmental clusters of 4× NVIDIA DGX Spark nodes (512 GB unified memory pool) alongside an
executive Master Core Apex Node. To support high-throughput tensor sharding and real-time RDMA (RoCEv2)
traffic, the physical network must be engineered across three operational scenarios with strict distance and hardware
parameters.
2. Scenario 1: The Local Data Room / Single Floor
Topology: Lossless Leaf-Spine Fabric utilizing high-density core switch mesh for sub-microsecond switch hops and
full line-rate 200G/400G RoCEv2 throughput.
  Physical Limitations & Signal Physics
     DAC Limits (≤ 3m to 5m): Copper twinax assemblies suffer high-frequency attenuation via skin effect and dielectric
     absorption. Beyond 5m, insertion loss causes unacceptable bit-error rates (BER).
     OM4 Multi-Mode Fiber (up to 100m): Bounded by modal dispersion, where different light modes arrive at skewed times,
     blurring optical pulses.

  Scenario 1 Hardware Bill of Materials (BOM)
     Switches: NVIDIA Spectrum-4 SN5600 (32-port 400GbE / 64-port 200GbE) with hardware-accelerated RoCEv2 and
     PFC/ECN.
     Adapters: NVIDIA ConnectX-7 Dual-Port 200G/400G Adapters with GPUDirect RDMA.
     Cabling: 200G/400G DAC Twinax (≤3m); NVIDIA 400GBASE-SR4 QSFP112 transceivers with OM4 MMF trunk patch
     cords (5m–100m).

3. Scenario 2: Large Building / Campus Complex
Topology: Hierarchical Spine-Leaf Campus Fabric bridging MDF and IDF frames across wings or separate campus
structures.
  Physical Limits & BOM
     Attenuation & Dispersion: OS2 Single-Mode Fiber (SMF) limits unamplified FR4 optics to 2 km and LR4 optics to 10 km.
     Chromatic dispersion causes optical pulse smearing.
     Core BOM: Juniper PTX10001-36MR routers, NVIDIA Spectrum-4 modular switches, 400GBASE-FR4/LR4 QSFP-DD
     transceivers, and OS2 armored trunk cables.
     Clock Sync: Microchip TimeProvider 4100 Grandmaster Clocks for PTP (IEEE 1588) sub-microsecond timing alignment.

4. Scenario 3: Geographically Distributed (Global WAN)
Topology: Hub-and-Spoke Enterprise WAN Architecture with asynchronous DCI links connecting regional clusters to
the Master Core Apex Node.
  Physical Limits & BOM
     Light Propagation Floor: Light travels at ~200,000 km/s in glass, creating an immutable latency floor of ~10ms RTT per
     1,000 km. Real-time RoCE memory pooling is impossible; asynchronous state handoffs are required.
     Master & DCI BOM: NVIDIA DGX H200/B200 Apex nodes, Ciena Waveserver 5 coherent transponders, ROADMs, and
     EDFA optical line amplifiers.

6. Critical Issues & Pitfalls to Avoid
  Operational Safeguards
     PFC Deadlocks (Scenario 1): Misconfigured Priority Flow Control freezes memory buses. Fix: Always enforce ECN
     alongside PFC.
     Fiber Bend Radius (Scenario 2): Pulling OS2 fiber tighter than 20x outer diameter causes macro-bending signal loss.
     WAN Synchronous Locking (Scenario 3): Attempting raw RoCEv2 or cluster memory paging across city WANs causes
     catastrophic timeout stalls. Fix: Use asynchronous JSON payloads and compressed KV-cache prefetching.
